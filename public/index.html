        See your bare-shell house finished – before you build

        Shell to Home turns a phone photo of a bare-shell room or house (bare brick, exposed concrete, empty floors, open ceilings) into a finished, modern home: flooring, wall paint, ceiling, lighting, fixtures and furniture. It is made for homeowners and small contractors who want to try ideas before spending money.

        Your style, your references

        Pick a style – modern minimalist, Scandinavian, luxury contemporary, Indian modern or industrial loft – or add up to five reference images and tell the app what each one controls: overall style, flooring, kitchen, colour palette or furniture.

        Before and after

        Drag the slider to compare the bare room with the finished one, or view them side by side. Change only the floor, the paint or the kitchen cabinets and everything else stays the same.

        3D walkthrough and video

        Your rooms become a 3D house you can walk through in the browser. Download the walkthrough as an MP4 video to share with family or your builder, or the 3D model as a GLB file.

        Free, private and open source

        The preview runs entirely in your browser – no sign-up, no uploads. The source code is on GitHub under the MIT licence, with optional photo-real AI renders when you self-host it with your own API key.

        Questions

        Is it really free?

        Yes. The preview, 3D walkthrough and MP4 export cost nothing. Photo-real AI renders are optional and use your own OpenAI or Google Gemini key in the self-hosted version.

        Are my photos uploaded?

        No. They stay in this browser. Only if you self-host with an AI engine and choose it are they sent – to that provider only.

        What photos work best?

        Stand in the doorway, face the far wall straight on and hold the phone level, in daylight or with the lights on. Then use “Adjust room box” to line up the far wall.

        Can I use it for a full house exterior?

        Yes. Choose “Full house exterior” as the room type to see the facade rendered and painted, with windows, a front door, a paved path, planting and outdoor lights.

        Is this a construction plan?

        No. Images, videos and 3D models are visual previews with rough size estimates. Check anything structural, electrical or plumbing with a qualified professional.
