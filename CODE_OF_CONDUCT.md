# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their experience, background, identity or level of technical knowledge. Many people here are homeowners and builders, not programmers, and every question is welcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give constructive feedback about ideas and code, not about people.
- Accept that people have different tastes in design: there is no "wrong" style.
- Respect privacy: don't share other people's photos or homes without permission.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information (addresses, photos of their home) without consent.
- Spam, or repeatedly derailing discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and code, and may block contributors who break this code of conduct. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile ([@roshanshaji786](https://github.com/roshanshaji786)). Reports are handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
